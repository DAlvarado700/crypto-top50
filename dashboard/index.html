Fear & Greed Index

BTC dominance / total market cap

No free historical source exists for this -- it accumulates one point per day starting from when this feature shipped (and once deployed, since the daily job runs on GitHub Actions, not this machine).

Stablecoin supply trend

Aggregate market cap of tracked stablecoins, as a rough "money on the sidelines" liquidity proxy -- not on-chain flow data.

Altcoin Season Index

% of that day's top 50 (excluding BTC and stablecoins) whose trailing 90-day return beat BTC's over the same window. ≥75% is conventionally "altcoin season", ≤25% is "Bitcoin season". Needs at least 90 days of history before the first point appears, and at least 10 eligible coins on a given day to report a value.

Compare two coins (% return since entry)

Each coin is rebased to 0% at its own most recent top-50 entry date, so coins that entered at very different times are still comparable on the same x-axis (days since entry).
